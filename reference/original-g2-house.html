Four things in the sketches to settle before construction drawings

1. The stair to the second floor starts inside Bedroom 2. On the first-floor sketch the stair up begins at column 7, rows 31 to 33, and the only way to reach it is through the 2nd bedroom. Anyone going to the office or terrace walks through that bedroom. The model shows it as drawn.
2. That same stair is too short to climb comfortably. The ground-floor stair has 6′ of straight run before the turn (columns 4 to 9). The first-floor stair has only 3′ (columns 7 to 9). At a 10′ floor height and 7.5″ risers, the upper flight ends up with treads about 7″ deep, which is very steep. Starting it at column 4, as on the ground floor, fixes this but removes the 4′ × 4′ space beside the wardrobes.
3. The stair passes over the kitchen platform. The second flight climbs above columns 10 to 12, rows 25 to 30. Above the rear end of the platform the underside of the stair is only about 5′ to 6′ from the floor, so there is no room for wall cabinets or a chimney there. The model keeps the hob and chimney at the front end (rows 23 to 24), where the ceiling is full height.
4. The stairs are reached through the kitchen. From the main hall you go through the glass slider, along the kitchen, and through the rear kitchen door to reach the stair. Guests going to the first-floor bedrooms pass through the kitchen.

Questions for you

  1. Is it acceptable that the second-floor stair is entered through Bedroom 2, or should that corner become a shared lobby with the bedroom door moved?
  2. May I start the first-floor stair at column 4 so both stairs are identical and comfortable?
  3. What floor-to-floor height do you plan? I used 10′ (9′6″ clear).
  4. Which direction does the road face? This decides the temple orientation, sunlight in the balcony and where the terrace needs shade.
  5. Second floor, columns 9 to 12, rows 27 to 37 are blank in the sketch. I treated rows 25 to 33 as the open stairwell and rows 34 to 37 as a lobby with the rear window. Is that right?
  6. First-floor rear washroom: the sketch shows no door. I placed it on the side facing the window bay. It also has no window or ventilator in the rear wall. Should I add one?
  7. Is there any open margin or parking space between the road and the main door, or does the house start at the plot line?
  8. Second-floor office pantry: is a counter with sink, an induction hob and an under-counter fridge enough, or do you want a gas connection and a full-height fridge?
  9. Is the water tank in the wash yard an underground sump, or an above-ground tank as drawn?
